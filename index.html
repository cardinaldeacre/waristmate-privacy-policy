Privacy Policy

How WaristMate collects, uses, and protects the information you entrust to it while calculating and recording Islamic inheritance (Faraidh). Last updated: July 21, 2026

Contents
    Contents

      Welcome to WaristMate — an Islamic inheritance (Faraidh) management and calculation application designed to help you calculate inheritance distribution and securely manage related records. Your privacy matters to us, so this policy explains, in plain terms, what we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the rights you hold over your own information.

      01

      Information We Collect

      a. Account Information

      When you create an account, we may collect:

      • Name
      • Email address
      • Authentication information through Google Sign-In (if used)

      b. User Data

      WaristMate allows you to create and manage inheritance-related information, including:

      • Asset information
      • Debt information
      • Will (Wasiat) information
      • Heir information
      • Personal inheritance records
      • Notes related to inheritance planning

      c. Device Information

      For security and troubleshooting purposes, we may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

      • Device model
      • Operating system version
      • Application version
      • Crash reports
      We do not collect your precise location.
      02

      How We Use Your Information

      We use your information to:

      • Provide inheritance calculation services
      • Synchronize your data across devices
      • Secure your account
      • Improve application performance
      • Fix bugs and technical issues
      • Respond to support requests
      We do not use your data for advertising purposes.
      03

      Data Storage

      Your account information and application data are stored securely using cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase.

      Sensitive user data is protected using End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) wherever applicable, ensuring that protected information remains confidential during storage and transmission.

      04

      Data Sharing

      We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

      Your information is only shared with trusted service providers necessary to operate the application, such as:

      • Authentication services
      • Secure cloud database services
      • Application infrastructure providers

      These providers are required to maintain appropriate security measures.

      05

      Data Security

      We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

      However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

      06

      Data Retention

      Your data is retained as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide our services.

      You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

      07

      Your Rights

      Depending on your applicable laws, you may have the right to:

      • Access your personal data
      • Correct inaccurate information
      • Delete your account and associated data
      • Withdraw consent where applicable
      08

      Children's Privacy

      WaristMate is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

      09

      Third-Party Services

      WaristMate may use trusted third-party services including:

      • Supabase
      • Google Sign-In (if enabled)

      Each third-party service has its own privacy practices.

      10

      Changes to This Privacy Policy

      We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

      Any updates will be published within the application or through the official Privacy Policy page. Continued use of WaristMate after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

      11

      Contact Us

      If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official support email associated with WaristMate.
